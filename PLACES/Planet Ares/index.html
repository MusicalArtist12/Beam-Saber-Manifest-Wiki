    Cities and notable locations

    City of Freyja

    This sea to our West, the Argyre sea, is about 3/4 frozen over in the middle of winter, and almost fully thawed toward the end of summer (though with a number of icebergs still floating around even then) This is near a magnetic pole, so aurora are common above Freyja
    And Freyja is an Ephraim owned city, but with many imported SarCo businesses and civilians making up around a quarter of the population. Freyja is roughly Seattle-metropolitan-area sized and similarly spread out into a number of boroughs that are basically just loosely attached cities with pockets of forest between them.

    Argent Logistics Base

    Argent Logistics is located in an abandoned airport on the outskirts of the city of Freyja.

    Westside Workers Workshop

    The Westside Workers are excellent mechanics, engineers, and builders. Businessman and head mechanic Roman lives there with his daughter Xiaohua. They have been contracted to build the 20 mile long bridge between Freyja and Efra.

    Mount Olympus

    The tallest mountain on Ares. At its highest elevation, it pierces the exosphere Home to The Heroes of Ares and site of the Gravity Rail. The Heroes' base, The Threshold, stands atop the peak. A small city is nestled at the foot of the mountain, around the base of the Gravity Rail. There is an excellent local pizza shop.
